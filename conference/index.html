KZNDHC 28th Annual Medical Conference

28th Annual Medical Conference

2 - 4 August 2024

Coastlands Musgrave, Durban

About the Conference

KZNDHC is pleased to announce our 28th Annual Medical Conference. This is a face-to-face conference scheduled for 2-4 August 2024 at Coastlands Musgrave. This event is CPD accredited and open to all GPs registered with the HPCSA. Registration details will follow closer to the time.

Conference Details

Date: 2-4 August 2024

Venue: Coastlands Musgrave, Durban

Accreditation: CPD accredited for all GPs

Open to: All GPs and healthcare professionals
