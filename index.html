Who We Are

About KZNDHC

For over three decades, KZNDHC has been the unified voice of doctors across KwaZulu-Natal, championing quality healthcare for all.

We represent medical professionals from all disciplines, working together to shape a healthcare system that serves every citizen with dignity, respect, and the highest standards of care. Our coalition is built on collaboration, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to ethical medical practice.

  • Representing doctors across all disciplines
  • Partnerships with leading healthcare institutions
  • Dedicated member support and advocacy

Our Purpose

Our Mission

KZNDHC's mission statement is to promote and enhance quality, cost-effective, accessible healthcare to all citizens on the basis of sound economic principles without compromising ethical standards.

Our Mission

To create a common platform where all doctors can collectively promote, maintain, and enhance quality, cost-effective, and accessible healthcare for all citizens of KZN through sound economic principles without compromising ethical standards.

Our Vision

To be the leading healthcare coalition in KwaZulu-Natal, setting standards of excellence in medical practice and fostering a healthcare system that serves every citizen with dignity and quality.

What We Offer

What KZNDHC Can Do For You

We provide professional medical services and advice pertaining to the private practice sectors. The structured organisation of fantastic workshops, CMEs and conferences is what we provide, and we always act on behalf of the family practitioners regarding medical aid queries.

The KwaZulu-Natal Doctors Healthcare Coalition was brought into being during 1995, by a group of doctors who shared a common vision — to become the reference organisation in the private health care industry in the KwaZulu-Natal region.
