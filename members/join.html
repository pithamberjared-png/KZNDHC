Why Join KZNDHC?

KZNDHC is the largest representative GP organisation in the private sector in KZN. We are a member of, or represented on the following boards:

IPAFIndependent Practitioner Association Foundation
UFFPUnity Forum of Family Practitioners
ISQUAInternational Society for Quality in Health Care
NDOHNational Dept of Health
B4SABusiness 4 South Africa
OHSCOffice of Health Standards Compliance

Member Benefits

Quality CPD program and internationally recognised annual medical conference

Affiliation with national organisations - IPAF/UFFP

Newsbriefs and Newsletters at regular intervals

Offers and discounts with partners

Medical Aid news and updates

What Your Membership Helps Achieve

Be represented with Medical Aids and Department of Health

Drive issues relevant to the success of our medical careers

Be informed on notices, amendments, and law changes

Stand as a collective group against corporate intrusion

Protect our scope of practice
